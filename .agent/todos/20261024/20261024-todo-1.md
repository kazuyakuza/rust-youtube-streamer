confirmation budget (3801 frames ≈ 126 s at 30 fps, `speed=0.999x`) and **exited 0** — the RTMPS

  accept video-only?" unverified.

  YouTube's ingest signal lock even though the bytes arrive.

Operator facts recorded (user-supplied, 2026-10-24): the `made_for_kids` config option WORKED
